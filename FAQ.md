# Contests FAQ

<details><summary>Why is there no rank-selection menu?</summary>

Each Johto hall runs its own rank. Your selected Pokémon needs 0/1/2/3 wins in that category for NORMAL/SUPER/HYPER/MASTER. Wins can come from any rank. The hall checks eligibility after you choose the category and Pokémon.
</details>

<details><summary>What is condition versus sheen?</summary>

Condition measures preparation for one category. Sheen limits feeding over the Pokémon's lifetime. Ten snacks fill sheen; specialize instead of trying to maximize every category. A snack preview appears before spending money or capacity.
</details>

<details><summary>Can I withdraw? What happens to my party?</summary>

In Johto judging, press B at the move menu and confirm with A. The contest ends without a win and your full party returns. Appeals do not spend battle PP. Reloading an unfinished contest cancels it and restores the team instead of restarting the routine halfway through.
</details>

<details><summary>Spoilers: how do I get the MASTER challenge's reward?</summary>

Win a MASTER contest with Fantina as an opponent. She is guaranteed until that first victory, across all categories. Afterward she approaches in the lobby with one stone. If your bag is full, clear a slot and revisit a contest lobby. The Dusk Stone reward option must be on when you win; turning it off completes the challenge without that gift.
</details>

<details><summary>Spoilers: can I use another dex expansion for the MASTER challenge's Pokémon?</summary>

Bundled evolutions defaults to Auto: WoobleDex supplies HONCHKROW and MISMAGIUS when available, and Contests supplies each missing species. On explicitly selects our bundled versions. Off loads neither bundled species and uses WoobleDex, or a uniquely named target from another pack. Existing saved On/Off choices are preserved; select Auto to adopt the new default. Fully quit and relaunch after changing.

When WoobleDex supplies both HONCHKROW and MISMAGIUS and the setting is Auto or Off, Fantina gives WoobleDex's own Dusk Stone (`DUSK_STONE`), so you only ever see one kind. On On, or without WoobleDex, she gives the contest Dusk Stone (`KC_DUSK_STONE`), which evolves into our bundled species. A contest Dusk Stone you already have keeps working. Neither mod overwrites the other's item.

Existing bundled Pokémon are protected separately when their species is not loaded, including in WoobleDex's expanded PC boxes. Set Bundled evolutions to On and relaunch to restore them. They are never automatically converted into another pack's IDs. With On, both providers' species can coexist and WoobleSpawns can select both; Auto avoids those duplicates when WoobleDex supplies both species.
</details>

<details><summary>An NPC faces me, but nothing happens.</summary>

Open MODS → ERRS and include the error with your bug report. A swallowed script error can present as an unresponsive NPC. Include the loaded version and other enabled mods.
</details>

<details><summary>It still behaves like an older version.</summary>

Fully quit and relaunch after importing the new ZIP. Check the load banner/version in MODS. On iOS, remove the older downloaded ZIP from Files before downloading again.
</details>
